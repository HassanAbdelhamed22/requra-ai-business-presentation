SEMANTIC CONFLICT DETECTION

Detecting contradictions before they reach code.

Requra uses hybrid embedding similarity and logical rules to identify, classify, and resolve conflicting requirements across multiple sources.

1. Semantic Filter: Cosine similarity on pgvector embeddings selects candidate pairs.

2. LLM Classifier: Identifies contradictions, constraint overlaps, or scope boundary mismatches.

3. Deterministic Overrides: Algorithmic filters verify that orthogonal limits are treated as complementary rather than conflicts.

4. Analyst Action: Generates custom clarification questions and resolution options for the PM.

REQ-002 vs REQ-012CONTRADICTION (HIGH)

REQ-002: Password-reset links expire after 15 minutes.

REQ-012: Reset links must remain valid for at least 1 hour.
